# retained-history lifetime

The available retained matching history used for first-activity analytics.

Cohort=lifetime does not mean first ever in the person’s actual life or all data once accepted. Applied retention and erasure can remove earlier observations and change first activity, lifecycle and cohort dates. The term preserves that consequential limit when describing lifetime retention.

Related: [retention cohort](retention-cohort.md), [applied retention cutoff](applied-retention-cutoff.md).
